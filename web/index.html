Can you stay unpredictable?

Play 20 rounds of rock, paper, scissors against an AI that reads your hand through the webcam and learns your habits as you play. It locks in its move before you show yours, and proves it.

Against 249 people it had never seen, it won 42% of rounds and lost 27%.

Your camera feed stays on your device. Hand tracking runs in this page, and nothing is uploaded or stored.

    How do I know the AI does not cheat?

    Before each round the AI shows the SHA-256 fingerprint of its move joined to a random code. After you play, it reveals the move and the code. Run the revealed text through any SHA-256 tool and you get the fingerprint that was on screen before you moved, so the AI must have chosen first. The random code stops anyone from guessing the move by trying the three possible fingerprints.
